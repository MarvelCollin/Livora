Livora People: third party models

All models run on the device. No image, face or embedding leaves the phone.

   Source: https://github.com/opencv/opencv_zoo/tree/main/models/face_detection_yunet

2. mobilefacenet.tflite
   MobileFaceNet face embedding model, 112x112 input, 192 dimensional output.
   Architecture: Chen et al., MobileFaceNets, https://arxiv.org/abs/1804.07573
   Weights and TensorFlow implementation: https://github.com/sirius-ai/MobileFaceNet_TF (Apache License 2.0)
   TensorFlow Lite conversion: https://github.com/syaringan357/Android-MobileFaceNet-MTCNN-FaceAntiSpoofing (MIT License, Copyright (c) 2019 syaringan357)
   Distributed in this form by https://github.com/hugocornellier/face_detection_tflite (Apache License 2.0)
   Apache License 2.0: https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0

3. LiteRT (TensorFlow Lite runtime) runs both models. Apache License 2.0.
